
# Engineering Document Control & Configuration Management Toolkit

A portfolio demonstration of engineering document control, configuration management, revision tracking, and engineering change management practices used in aerospace and electronics manufacturing.

## Project Overview

This toolkit demonstrates how controlled engineering documents are registered, maintained, revised, approved, and tracked throughout their lifecycle.

All records use fictional engineering and manufacturing data.

## Repository Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| document-register.csv | Master document register containing document IDs, revisions, ownership, and status |
| revision-history.csv | Tracks revision history, change descriptions, dates, and approvals |
| engineering-change-tracker.csv | Tracks engineering change orders, proposed revisions, and approval status |

## Core Configuration Management Functions

### Document Registration
Maintain a centralized record of controlled engineering documents.

### Revision Control
Record document changes, revision history, approval status, and traceability.

### Engineering Change Management
Track engineering change orders (ECOs), proposed modifications, reviews, approvals, and implementation.

### Document Lifecycle Management
Manage documentation from initial creation through review, approval, release, revision, archival, and controlled disposition.

## Example Document Control Workflow

1. Engineering change is requested.
2. Affected documents are identified.
3. Proposed revisions are prepared.
4. Changes undergo review and approval.
5. Approved revisions are formally released.
6. Document registers and revision histories are updated.
7. Superseded documents are archived or disposed of according to retention requirements.
